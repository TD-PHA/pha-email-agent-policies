Disconnect QuickBooks Online

About this app: PHA Time Tracker is a private internal-use application operated by Prestige Home Automation Ltd. for its own business. It is not a publicly distributed app. Disconnecting revokes the app's OAuth grant, which immediately ends its access to the Operator's QuickBooks Online company.

How to disconnect

Three equivalent ways to revoke PHA Time Tracker's access to your QuickBooks Online company:

Option 1: In PHA Time Tracker (recommended)

  1. Sign in to PHA Time Tracker as a director and open Settings > QuickBooks.
  2. Press Disconnect and confirm.
  3. The app revokes the grant at Intuit and deletes its stored refresh token.

Option 2: Via QuickBooks Online settings

  1. Sign in to the QuickBooks Online company that authorised the app.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon) → Manage users → Apps (or Apps → My Apps).
  3. Find PHA Time Tracker.
  4. Click Disconnect.

Option 3: Via Intuit account dashboard

  1. Sign in to accounts.intuit.com.
  2. Go to Security → Connected apps.
  3. Locate PHA Time Tracker (the registered application name) in the list of authorised apps.
  4. Click Disconnect or Revoke access.

PHA Time Tracker is registered with Intuit as a separate app from the Operator's PHA Email Agent, so disconnecting one does not affect the other.

What happens after disconnect

  • The app's refresh token stops working immediately on Intuit's side, and the app can no longer read from or send mileage to QuickBooks Online.
  • If you disconnected in the app, the stored token is also deleted from Supabase Vault and the weekly keep-alive stops for that connection. If you disconnected from QuickBooks Online or the Intuit dashboard, the app's next attempt to use the token is refused by Intuit, and the app tells the directors that QuickBooks needs reconnecting.
  • The app keeps its own record of what it has already sent (which mileage, in which Bill or supplier credit, and when), because that is part of the Operator's accounting history.
  • No cleanup is needed in QuickBooks Online. Bills and supplier credits already entered remain in the company until the company owner changes or removes them.

Reconnect

To reconnect, a director opens Settings > QuickBooks and presses Connect QuickBooks (see the Connect page).
