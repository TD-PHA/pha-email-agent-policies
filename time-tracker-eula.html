PHA Time Tracker · Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Effective date: 2026-10-15

1. Software and Operator

"PHA Time Tracker" (the "Software") is internal software developed and operated by Prestige Home Automation Ltd. ("the Operator"), a company registered in England and Wales. The Software schedules work, records time and mileage, and reports job costs for the Operator. Through its connection to Intuit QuickBooks Online, it enters approved business mileage into the Operator's own QuickBooks Online company as Bills and supplier credits, and reads the expense accounts, VAT codes, suppliers and company information needed to do so.

2. Scope of Use

The Software is provided exclusively for the internal operations of Prestige Home Automation Ltd. It is not distributed to third parties, not licensed to external customers, and not offered as a commercial service. The QuickBooks Online connection is made and used only by the Operator's two directors, for the Operator's single QuickBooks Online company. Other users of the Software are the Operator's own staff, who have no access to the QuickBooks Online connection.

3. Authorisation Required Before Use

The Software accesses Intuit QuickBooks Online only with an OAuth grant explicitly given by a director in Intuit's own consent pages. The Software never sees the director's Intuit password. The refresh token is held encrypted in Supabase Vault on the Operator's own system, server-side, and is not stored in any browser or shared with any third party.
