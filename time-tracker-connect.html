Connect / Reconnect QuickBooks Online

About this app: PHA Time Tracker is a private internal-use application operated by Prestige Home Automation Ltd. for its own business. It is not a publicly distributed app and is not available on the Intuit App Marketplace. The connection described below is started by one of the Operator's two directors, inside the app, and authorises the Operator's own QuickBooks Online company.

How the connect flow works

  1. A director signs in to PHA Time Tracker and opens Settings > QuickBooks. Only directors can see this screen.
  2. The director presses Connect QuickBooks. The app sends the browser to Intuit's own sign-in and consent page (appcenter.intuit.com), asking for the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope only.
  3. The director signs in to Intuit and selects the Operator's QuickBooks Online company. The app never sees the director's Intuit password.
  4. Intuit returns the director to the app's server-side callback, which exchanges the authorisation code for tokens. The refresh token is stored encrypted in Supabase Vault on the Operator's system; it never reaches the browser.
  5. The app reads the connected company's name and shows it on Settings > QuickBooks, so the director can confirm the right company was chosen. If it is the wrong company, the director presses Disconnect at once.
  6. The director then chooses the expense account, the VAT code and each person's supplier that approved mileage will be entered under.

Keeping the connection alive

A weekly scheduled task refreshes the token, so the connection does not lapse after a quiet period. Settings > QuickBooks shows the date by which the connection must be renewed.

Reconnect flow

If the connection has been disconnected or has expired, a director opens Settings > QuickBooks again and presses Connect QuickBooks. Once a company has been connected, the app refuses a reconnection to a different company.
